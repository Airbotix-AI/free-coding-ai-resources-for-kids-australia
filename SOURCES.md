## Inclusion method

A resource needs a usable no-fee learning path or public educational reading, a primary source and a clearly described boundary. Conditional country eligibility, hardware, account requirements, API limits and uncertain access are not hidden. No time-limited trial is treated as an enduring free course. Free-reading directories may contain paid external links; those links are not automatically included.

Age suggestions marked editorial, example outputs and progression paths are this project’s judgements. They are not clinical age assessments, provider terms, school endorsements or guaranteed learning gains. Actual learning is shown by a finished artifact, an explanation and an improvement. Provider terms and school policies still govern account use.

Most resource pages were readable through official web sources. Teachable Machine’s main production page loaded with incomplete text in the checked browser; its Google-owned explanatory page was readable. It remains access-limited. AI for Oceans redirects to a current course-unit route; source reading is not a completed activity test.

The six corresponding Airbotix article bodies were opened in a browser on the review date. Public sibling repositories were checked through GitHub; private company repositories are not included.
